Welcome to Kavu

April 22, 2026·1 min

This site is an example garden built with Kavu, a Hugo theme for digital gardens and personal knowledge bases. The notes you see are short, generic samples meant to demonstrate the theme — feel free to delete them all and start your own garden in their place.

A few things to try right now:

  • Open the What Is a Digital Garden note and follow the links inside it. Notice the bracketed [[wikilink]] rendering — that’s How Wikilinks Work.
  • Scroll back to the homepage and look at the Network graph. Each dot is a note, coloured by maturity. Hover and click around; press / to search.
  • Check the colour-coded dots in the index. Green is evergreen, blue is growing, amber is a seedling. The system is explained in Note Maturity Stages.

If you’re starting fresh, the Seedling Example shows what a half-formed thought looks like — Kavu is built around the idea that publishing rough notes is fine.
